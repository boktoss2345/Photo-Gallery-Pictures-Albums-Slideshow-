Privacy

This app never uploads your photos. There are no network requests, no analytics and no cloud services. Imported images, thumbnails, albums and favorites are stored only in this browser’s IndexedDB database on this device.

Clearing your browser data, app data or site data will permanently remove your imported library from this app. Removing a photo from the library only deletes this app’s stored copy — it never deletes the original file on your device.

Albums created here are groups inside this app. They do not create folders on your device.
